# ACNH Wallpaper Assets

A collection and cataloging system for extracting, cataloging, and archiving high-resolution wallpapers and room showcase screenshots from Animal Crossing: New Horizons.

## Language

**Wallpaper**:
An in-game interior surface item applied to room walls in Animal Crossing: New Horizons.
_Avoid_: Wall, background, mural

**In-Game Screenshot**:
A full-room 16:9 visual capture (typically 1280x720) showcasing the wallpaper rendered on room walls in an interior environment.
_Avoid_: Big picture, live photo, appearance image

**Inventory Icon**:
A 2D square item sprite or catalog render representing the wallpaper item in inventory or storage.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, icon, preview

**Wall Texture**:
The raw flat 2D graphic texture (typically 1024x1024 or 2048x2048) extracted directly from the game package data that the game engine wraps around interior room surfaces.
_Avoid_: Raw image, datamine dump, skin

**Showcase Capture**:
A video frame extracted from a catalog or showcase video demonstrating the wallpaper applied in an in-game room.
_Avoid_: Video screenshot, rip
